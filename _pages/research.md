My work spans stochastic optimization and computational economics.

## Selected Research

### [Rationalizing Collective Revealed Preferences with an Application in Fair Resource Allocation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23985)

This work develops a surrogate-market framework for learning collective revealed preferences and studies its use in fair resource allocation. The paper is available as an [arXiv preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23985).

### Multistage Stochastic Optimization

Research on adaptive stochastic approximation for two-stage and multistage stochastic programs, including computational comparisons with Dynamic Stochastic Approximation (DSA) and related methods.

## Additional Research

### Tuning-Free First-Order Algorithms

Research on parameter-free methods for nonsmooth convex optimization, including convergence analysis for deterministic and stochastic settings and computational evaluation on L1 regression and SVM benchmarks.
